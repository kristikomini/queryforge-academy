How this works

Every idea is explained twice

A 🧒 Simple box — the version you would give a child — and a 🎓 Professional box — the version you would give an interviewer. The switch in the top bar hides one of them, which turns any chapter into a self-test: read the simple one, then produce the professional one from memory.

Progress means retrieval

A chapter is worth 25% for reading and 75% for the test score. Read all fifty-two and answer nothing and you are capped at 25%. That is deliberate: the number on the top bar should mean something.

Recognition and recall are different skills

Multiple choice measures recognition. An interview measures recall. So there is a viva that hides the answer until you have committed to one, and a simulator that adds a clock and takes the options away.

Nothing is behind a login

An account exists only to carry your progress between a laptop and a phone. Everything works signed out, offline, and from file://.

What this is not

⚠ Read this before you trust the numbers
  • The mastery percentage is a fact about your test scores on these questions. It is not a fact about your ability, and it is certainly not a fact about whether anyone will hire you.
  • The viva and the simulator are self-marked. That is the weakest link in this whole site and it is mitigated, not solved: your own words stay on screen next to the model answer, and the rule's code terms are matched literally so "you never mentioned SKIP LOCKED" is at least a fact. It still cannot tell whether you were right.
  • The leaderboard is a nudge, not an audit. Your progress document lives in your browser and you can edit it. The server re-derives the four numbers it shows rather than trusting what the browser claims, which raises the effort a little and proves nothing.
  • A lost recovery code is a lost account. There is no email address on file, so there is no way round it. That is a real cost of the no-personal-data decision and it is not worth pretending otherwise.
