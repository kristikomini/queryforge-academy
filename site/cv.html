CV builder

An Italian CV and cover letter, linted against the rules in chapter 49.

⚠ What this is, precisely

It is not an AI that writes your CV. It can tell you when a bullet contains no facts, when the GDPR consent line is missing, when a language level is not CEFR, and when the result will not fit on one page. It cannot tell you whether what you wrote is interesting. Every warning below cites the paragraph it comes from, and if it could not cite one it would not be here.
