Two kinds of word, and they are different problems.

Technical terms stay English

la query, il deploy, fare il merge, il backup, l'indice, il join. Translating them marks somebody who learned the language from a textbook rather than from a team. The trap is the small number that are translated — collaudo, avvio in produzione, presa in carico — where using the English word marks you just as clearly.

⚠ Process and contract vocabulary is where interviews are lost

It is entirely Italian, it appears in adverts and contracts, and nobody teaches it. A candidate who can tune a query but does not know what RAL, CCNL or apprendistato mean is at a real disadvantage in a conversation that decides their salary.
