Mock interview

A clock, no going back, and the two questions asked in every interview and prepared for in almost none: mi parli di lei and ha domande per noi?

Pick a round

Questions come from the rules deck and from the question bank — with the options stripped off. That turns a recognition item into a recall item at no content cost, which is the entire trick of this page.

What this adds that the other pages do not
  • A clock, about ninety seconds a question. Interviews are timed and thinking silently for four minutes is not an option you have.
  • No going back. You cannot revise an answer once you have moved on, because you cannot in the room either.
  • The non-technical parts. Every round opens with mi parli di lei and closes with ha domande per noi? — the two that decide more outcomes than any query.
⚠ It produces a transcript, not a score

Deliberately. A self-marked percentage is a number about your generosity. What you said, next to what the course says, with the time you took, is not — and it is something you can reread the evening before the real thing.
