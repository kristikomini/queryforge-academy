Mock exam

A timed, mixed selection from the whole bank. Pass mark 80% — the same bar a chapter test uses, and roughly the bar a screening test uses.

Choose a length

Questions are drawn at random from every chapter, including ones you have not read. That is the point: a screening test does not restrict itself to the syllabus you have covered so far.

What the exam does and does not do

Exam answers do not enter the spaced-repetition schedule. It is an assessment, not a study session, and mixing the two would let a bad exam day reset intervals on cards you know perfectly well. It records your best score, your attempt count, and 2 XP per correct answer.
