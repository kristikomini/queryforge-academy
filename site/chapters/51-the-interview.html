The interview

Mi parli di lei, the technical round, the questions to ask them, and how to talk about money.

From the advert

“Buone capacita comunicative” — the least technical requirement on the list, and the one that decides the outcome once the technical bar is met.

“Mi parli di lei”

🧒 Simple

The first question is always the same, everybody knows it is coming, and almost nobody prepares for it. It is not a test. It is them handing you two minutes to set the agenda — and if you do not set it, they will.

🎓 A structure that works, in ninety seconds
  1. Where you are now, in one sentence.
  2. What you can do, with one concrete example — not a list.
  3. Why you are here, specific to this company.

Note what it does: names a concrete artefact, gives a reason for a technical choice, and ends by quoting their advert back at them. It also hands the interviewer three obvious follow-up questions, all of which you can answer.

Answering about a gap

✔ Own it, bound it, bridge it — in three sentences

“No, I have not administered a production instance — I have not been on call for one. I understand the concepts: recovery models, the difference between RPO and RTO, why a replica is not a backup. What I have not done is a real restore under pressure, and I would want somebody to walk me through the first one.”

Honest, precise about the boundary, and it demonstrates knowledge while admitting the gap. Every interviewer has hired somebody who overclaimed; the memory is vivid and it is what they are screening for.

⚠ The three answers that end an interview quietly
  • Bluffing. One follow-up question exposes it, and now everything else you said is in doubt.
  • “I don't know” with nothing after it. Add the bridge: how you would find out, or the nearest thing you do know.
  • Criticising a previous employer or their stack. Interviewers hear a preview of how you will talk about them.

The technical round

🎓 Answer in two layers, always

The fact, then the why. “A clustered index stores the rows in key order — so the primary key choice is a physical storage decision, and every non-clustered index carries that key as its row locator, which is why a wide key makes them all bigger.” The first clause is the answer; the second clause is the offer. This is the single highest-leverage habit in a technical interview and it works for every question in this course.

They askThey are really asking
“What is an index?”Do you understand it as a data structure with costs, or as a magic speed-up?
“What is a transaction?”Have you thought about failure and concurrency, or only the happy path?
“How do you optimise a query?”Do you have a method, or a bag of tricks?
“Tell me about a difficult bug”How do you investigate, and do you say what you got wrong?
“Where do you see yourself in five years?”Will you still be here in two?
When you genuinely do not know

“I do not know that. My guess would be X, because Y — but I would check before relying on it.” A reasoned guess, clearly labelled as a guess, is worth almost as much as the answer and considerably more than silence. What it demonstrates is the thing they actually need: that you can reason about unfamiliar territory without pretending.

“Ha domande per noi?”

⚠ “No, I think you covered everything” is a bad answer

It reads as low interest, and it wastes the only part of the interview where you are evaluating them. Have five prepared; two will already have been answered.

✔ Questions that are also signals
  • “How do schema changes reach production? Are migrations versioned?” — tells you a lot, instantly.
  • “Do you have a restore drill? When was the last one?” — the answer is often a pause, and that is information.
  • “Who owns the database — is there a DBA, or is it shared by the team?”
  • “What does the first month look like? What would I be working on?”
  • “What is the largest customer database, roughly? How many rows in the biggest table?”
  • “What would make you say, in six months, that hiring me went well?”

Every one of those is a real question and a demonstration that you know what matters. That double duty is what makes them worth preparing.

Talking about money

🎓 The Italian specifics you need
  • RAL = retribuzione annua lorda: gross annual salary, before tax and contributions. Every figure discussed is RAL unless stated otherwise.
  • It is paid in 13 or 14 mensilità — the RAL is the annual total, so the monthly figure is RAL ÷ 13 or ÷ 14, not ÷ 12.
  • Net is roughly 60–70% of RAL for these ranges. If you are given a net figure, ask for the RAL.
  • The CCNL — usually metalmeccanico or commercio — sets the minimum, the level, the notice period and holidays. Asking which one applies is a normal, informed question.
  • Buoni pasto (meal vouchers), welfare aziendale, training budget and remote-work days are all real, negotiable, and often easier for them to move than RAL.
✔ When they ask first, and they will

“La mia aspettativa è nella fascia 28–32k RAL, in base al ruolo complessivo. Mi interessa anche capire formazione e possibilità di crescita. Qual è il range previsto per questa posizione?” — a range, a reason, a note that money is not the only variable, and the question handed straight back. Naming a range is better than refusing to answer; refusing reads as evasive in an Italian recruiter call, where the RAL question is routine and expected early.

On the offer

Ask for it in writing with the CCNL level, the RAL, the number of mensilità, the contract type (indeterminato, determinato, apprendistato) and the start date. Apprendistato is common and normal for a junior in Italy: lower cost to the employer, mandatory training, and it converts to indeterminato — worth understanding rather than being alarmed by. Take a day. Nobody withdraws an offer because you asked for twenty-four hours.

Afterwards

  • Write down every question you were asked, the same day. It is the single best study list you will ever have, and it is gone by tomorrow.
  • A short thank-you email is uncommon in Italy and costs nothing. Two sentences; reference one specific thing discussed.
  • If you are rejected, ask for feedback once, politely. Perhaps a third answer, and that third is worth more than a week of study.
  • Rejection at this level is usually not about you. The role changed, an internal candidate appeared, the budget moved. Send the next application the same week.

  4. A reasoned guess labelled as a guess beats silence and beats bluffing.
  5. Never criticise a previous employer or their stack.

Interview questions this chapter answers

Mi parli di lei.

Ninety seconds, three parts: where you are now, what you can do with one concrete example rather than a list, and why this company specifically — quoting something from their advert or their product. It should hand them three follow-up questions you can answer.

Quali sono le sue aspettative economiche?

Give a range in RAL with a brief reason, note that training and growth also matter, and hand the question back by asking their budgeted range. Refusing to name a number reads as evasive in an Italian recruiter call, where this question comes early and is routine.

Ha domande per noi?

Always yes. How schema changes reach production and whether migrations are versioned; whether there is a restore drill and when the last one was; who owns the database; what the first month looks like. Each is a genuine question and each demonstrates that you know what matters.
