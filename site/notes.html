How to make a note

On any chapter page, select a sentence and press 📌 Note this. The quote is captured and you write the paraphrase — because the paraphrase is what does the work. Notes are positioned against the document rather than the viewport, so one pinned beside a paragraph is still beside it tomorrow, at a different window size, on a different machine.
