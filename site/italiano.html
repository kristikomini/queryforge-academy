Phrases for a technical interview and a working day, plus what you would actually say about each topic. The technical nouns stay English on purpose.

The rule that governs all of this

Technical terms stay English — la query, il deploy, fare il merge, l'indice, il backup. Translating them marks somebody who learned the language from a textbook rather than from a team. The trap is the small number that genuinely are Italian: collaudo (acceptance testing), avvio in produzione (go-live), presa in carico (taking a ticket), segnalazione (a reported issue). Using the English word for those marks you just as clearly.

What this is not

⚠ It is not an Italian course

It assumes you already speak enough Italian to hold the conversation, and fills in the specific vocabulary of a technical interview and a development team — which is a small, learnable set that general language courses never cover. If you are starting from zero, this is the wrong tool and no amount of it will substitute for the language.
